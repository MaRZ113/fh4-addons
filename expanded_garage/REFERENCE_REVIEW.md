# Local source review

The four local checkouts were inspected at the paths below. This work uses the Driver: San Francisco repository as an external source reader and uses the workspace's Phase 4–7 readers for FH4 validation. The writer uses the PC wire layouts corroborated by local stock assets and ForzaTech Studio.

| Project | Original data | Mathematical conversion and generated resources | Target libraries and residual host data | FH4 applicability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FH1 ReImagined (GPL-3.0) | `tools/fm4_cars.py` resolves FM4 base/DLC DB rows, vehicle/wheel Carbins, textures, thumbnail, MAXData and audio. | `fm4_carbin.py` repacks shaders' vertex attributes and expands a packed damage direction + signed magnitude into FH1's packed displacement. Geometry and material names are clustered for new lamp records. `fm4_physdef.py` fits a support-plane convex hull and uses original scaled MAXData spheres. `Retune` fits FH1 fields on matching stock cars. The builder emits strings, stock/upgrade DB rows, manifest, stripped Carbins and mounts. | Normal synthesis partly uses common FH1 resources. Collision rigid-body fields are scaled from the closest FH1 vehicle, some car attributes come from FH1, and audio can resolve shared or fallback timbres. | Source organization, conservative emitter recognition, support hull and controlled pairing apply. Xbox shader patching, the inline 32-byte FH1 light records, FH1 physics blobs and FH1 catalogue regression coefficients do not carry to FH4 Steam. |
| carbin-garage (GPL-3.0) | `orchestrator/importwc.nim` preserves source ZIPs and LZX-decodes FM4 Carbin, XDS and companion data. `core/carbin/parser.nim` exposes sections; `core/cardb.nim` builds per-car DB snippets. | `core/carbin/emitter.nim` and `vertex_quat.nim` decode section-relative geometry, normals and material-local UVs to glTF. `core/xds.nim` decodes textures. The port layers build typed rewrites, textures and ZIPs. | `orchestrator/portto.nim` explicitly rewrites a destination donor ZIP. Preserving a donor archive's entries, authored fields, and metadata is part of that tool's working model. | Useful as a second reader and visual comparator. The donor-rewrite path and FM4/FH1 LZX packing are outside this FH4 writer. |
| ForzaTech Studio (MIT) | `ForzaTools.Bundles/Bundle.cs` and the model, mesh, skeleton, material, buffer and layout blobs expose the PC bundle contract. The services read lights, presets, Carbin, swatches and physics definitions. | `ModelBuilderService.cs` plus `GeometryProcessingService.cs` synthesize static Grub bundles from meshes. `CarbinEditorViewModel.Writing.cs` serializes typed scenes. The Modelbin conversion service adjusts destination layouts. | The static generator authors a neutral root and binds shared material paths. Scene and physics editors frequently start from parsed stock data. | FH4 Grub 1.1, v1.9 Mesh, v1.2 Modl, v5/v16 Carbin and shared material paths are direct references. Generic creation examples leave vehicle-specific animation, collision, controller dispatch and effect routing to the caller. |
| driversf-forza (MIT) | `formats/carbin.py` decodes FM4 pools, LODs, UVs and primitive topology. `formats/forzazip.py` resolves method 21; `formats/forza_audio.py` connects CMT/ET/HT to FSB loops. `build/fm4_dlc.py` stages DLC DB patches. | The conversion modules derive wheel fit, wheel blur, cockpit, geometry and texture projections. `build/handling.py` reads stock engine, gear ratios and torque curves then fits DSF parameters. The damage code maps source surfaces to DSF damage roles. `convert/engine_sound.py` builds DSF sample/bank and playback data. | DSF operations depend on stock host structures such as VO3D, damage slots and vehicle attachment schema. DSF sound packaging uses its own target structures. | FM4 parsing, source-chain tracing and evaluation methodology apply. DSF coordinate conventions, host damage mapping, handling formulas and its sound bank formats are destination-specific. |

FH1 source and carbin-garage are references only. Their implementation is neither copied into the new package nor linked as a dependency. The external Driver: San Francisco checkout stays under its own license. ForzaTech Studio's MIT license is included alongside the format work. Keep all extracted game data local.
