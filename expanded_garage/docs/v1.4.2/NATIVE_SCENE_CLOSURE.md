# Native scene resource closure

The generated 83-member Suzuki ZIP names its root Carbin `eg_suz_sx4_11.carbin`. The serialized scene identity is 12000, and each local Modelbin and skeleton reference resolves. The same compiler produced matching root identities and no missing local Modelbin paths for Honda 12002 and RUF 12001. Prior audits cover the checked 0x1123 offsets, ZIP/manifest and owned GameDB joins. The ordinary process opening the right archive confirms the physical mount is reachable. A mounted archive does not establish reads of its members.

All six archived controls have the resources below. None of the six are present in the generated cars. The matrix separates static consumer evidence from unknown first-view requirements. It does not classify the missing files as the cause of the MegaTexture failure.

The readback inspected 75 Suzuki Modelbin bounds, 920 render meshes and 685 shadow meshes. Bounds are finite and ordered, mesh position/UV transforms finite, and 18 distinct direct shared Materialbin members resolve. The regular static verifier also checks VLay descriptors, vertex/index stream ranges, scene bindings, physics and rig containers. It does not establish shader pass compatibility, deep texture loads or valid in-game camera state.

Suzuki's largest observed UV1 endpoint is about `13931.23`; it is already present in the indexed FM4 `bumperRb` near-source draws for `black` and `frame`. Presence in the source distinguishes it from an isolated FH4 arithmetic overflow. Its consumption by the FH4 material pass, active variant and relevance to MegaTexture remain unobserved. Auxiliary UV2–4 and colors remain compiler defaults. No blanket UV clamping was performed.

The generated `CameraOverrides` records have five target-only NULLs, populated in all 762 native records in the pinned baseline. Two max-height offsets are zero in 759 baseline records, and no-wheel cockpit Z is nonzero in 757. The values therefore require field-specific rules. Current evidence does not connect these fields to the Autoshow producer's RDX input.
